# Claude Farm

**Claude Farm has complete control over every aspect of Sol's environment.**

A programmable intelligence layer that observes, coordinates, and optimizes activity across Sol.  
Built for automation, monitoring, and autonomous decision making.

---

## ✨ Overview

Claude Farm acts as the command center for Sol's digital terrain.

It aggregates signals, evaluates conditions, and triggers actions across integrated systems — giving builders a unified way to manage complex on-chain and off-chain workflows.

Think of it as infrastructure for autonomous operations.

---

## 🧠 Core Principles

- **Total Awareness** – Understand every moving piece.
- **Deterministic Control** – Predictable execution.
- **Composable** – Plug into anything.
- **Autonomous** – Reduce human overhead.

---

## ⚙️ What Claude Farm Can Do

- Monitor environmental states  
- Automate reactions  
- Coordinate agents  
- Execute predefined or AI-driven strategies  
- Provide traceability for every action  

---

## 🏗 Architecture

```
Sensors → Indexer → Decision Engine → Executors → Reports
```

Claude Farm continuously loops through:

1. Collect  
2. Evaluate  
3. Decide  
4. Act  
5. Record
